Forecasts that tell you when they can be trusted.

Psephos forecasts elections and world events using several independent methods at once — statistical models, polls, markets, and research. When they all agree beyond 95%, it certifies a CALL. When they don't, it says so honestly: the question is open, and here is what would settle it. Every forecast is hash-stamped before the event and scored after — misses included.

How it works

1Independent methodsEach question is answered by instruments that can't fail the same way: structural models that read no news, poll averages corrected for measured pollster bias, betting markets, and a research pass. Their errors are measured, not assumed.
2The gateA CALL is issued only when the instruments independently clear 95%. Otherwise the verdict is WATCH — genuinely open — with tripwires naming exactly what would change it. Safety rules (like a new party with no electoral history) can suppress calls entirely.
3A provable recordEvery forecast is SHA-256 hashed the moment it's made and anchored into the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps, then scored once the event resolves. The track record cannot be quietly edited — by anyone, including us.

The public Ask ledger — this instrument scores itself

Any estimate can be saved to a shared, anonymous ledger — question, probability, date; no name. The weekly autopilot run checks every saved estimate past its resolution date against public sources and scores it. Hits and misses both stay on the page. No other answer box keeps its own report card.
